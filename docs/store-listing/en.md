**Full description (≤ 4000)**

Lalabuba is a free coloring page maker for kids: type any word and get a new coloring page in seconds. Color it by number, tap to fill, or draw freely with the pencil. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed.

A unicorn jumping over a rainbow, a dragon robot, a monster truck, a cat with a ball of yarn: whatever your child imagines, Lalabuba draws it as a clean black-and-white coloring page. Prefer something ready? Pick from hundreds of free coloring pages for kids: animals, dinosaurs, princesses, cars, rockets, and Christmas pictures in December.

HOW IT WORKS
• Type anything you want to color
• Tap a numbered area, then a color to fill it
• Draw your own details with the pencil
• Print the picture or save it to your gallery
• Challenge a friend: share the same picture and race to finish

EASY TO HARD
• 4 levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme
• Easy pages have big areas for little hands; hard pages keep older kids busy
• Color by number with 6 to 24 colors
• Crayon, pastel and nature palettes, plus palettes to unlock

REWARDS THAT KEEP KIDS CREATING
• 30+ stickers to collect: milestones, day streaks, explorer, creativity, sharing
• A new daily mission every day
• Unlock crayon packs: Neon, Candy and Galaxy
• Dress up your own penguin mascot

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• 100% free: no ads, no in-app purchases
• No account needed; an optional parent account keeps progress across devices
• Every subject and every picture is checked for child safety before it is shown
• External links sit behind a parental gate
• Works on phones and tablets, in 12 languages

Coloring pages for kids aged 3 to 12, and for anyone who likes to color.
