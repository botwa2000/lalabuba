Delete your account and data

Lalabuba: Kids Coloring · last updated November 2026

Lalabuba works without an account. If a grown-up created a family account (sign-in with an email code), you can delete it, and everything that belongs to it, at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Settings (a grown-up passes the parental check).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes the family account immediately, together with its child profiles, linked devices and sign-in sessions. It also deletes this device's community profile, shared artworks, ratings and usage statistics.

On the website

Sign in at lalabuba.com, open ⚙️ → your account, and tap Delete account twice.

By email

If you can't open the app or the website, write from the account's email address to info@lalabuba.com with the subject "Delete my Lalabuba account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: email address, child nicknames and avatars, linked devices, sign-in sessions, community profile, shared artworks, ratings, and the usage statistics recorded for the device.
  • Kept, without your account: nothing that identifies you. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example "120 dragons were drawn this week") cannot be traced back to you and are deleted after at most 13 months. Pictures you saved on your own device stay on your device.
